Common questions

  • Restoring a purchase on a new device. Open the friend picker (the round button at the top right of any scene), tap the grown-ups button, answer the question, then tap Restore. Purchases are tied to the Apple ID that bought them.
  • Why is there a math question? Dusty is made for young children. Anything that can spend money or leave the app sits behind a grown-ups gate, as Apple requires for kids' apps.
  • Sound or music is off. Grown-ups can adjust music, sound effects and story volume in Settings inside the app.
  • Family Sharing. Friends bought by one family member can be shared with the family group where Apple allows it.
