Privacy Policy

Dusty the Digger · Effective date: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Funloop LLC ("Funloop", "we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with the Dusty the Digger mobile application (the "App"). The short version: Dusty does not collect personal information from anyone, and it does not send information off your device.

Who this is for

Dusty the Digger is an interactive storybook made for young children and is intended to be used with a parent or guardian. This policy is written for the parents and guardians of those children, and for anyone else who uses the App.

Who we are

  • Company: Funloop LLC
  • App: Dusty the Digger (children's interactive storybook)
  • Contact: privacy@funloop.com
  • Site: funloop.com

What we collect

Nothing. The App has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, photos, contacts, location, microphone or camera access, or device identifiers. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits. The App runs entirely on your device and makes no network connections of its own.

In-app purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases that unlock additional friends for Dusty. These purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Funloop never receives your payment card details or your Apple ID. When a purchase or a restore completes, Apple tells the App which items your Apple ID owns, and the App keeps a note of that on the device so the content stays unlocked. Every purchase and every restore sits behind a grown-ups gate (a simple arithmetic question) so that a child cannot make a purchase alone. Apple's own handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

None of this is sent to Funloop or to anyone else. Deleting the App deletes it.

Children's privacy

Dusty the Digger is designed for children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information at all, it does not collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly receive any. There are no links out of the App and no advertising. Grown-up areas (settings, information pages, and purchases) sit behind a parental gate. If you believe we have somehow received personal information from a child, email privacy@funloop.com and we will delete it.

How we share information

We have nothing to share, and we do not sell personal information. The only third party involved is Apple, which processes in-app purchases and provides the App Store.

Security

Because the App keeps only a few preferences on your device and transmits nothing, there is no Funloop server holding your information. Purchase records are protected by Apple's App Store systems.

Your rights and choices

  • Manage purchases, refunds and Family Sharing through your Apple ID settings and the App Store.
  • Remove all locally stored preferences by deleting the App.
  • Ask us anything about this policy at privacy@funloop.com.

Changes to this policy

If the App ever starts collecting information, we will update this policy first, change the effective date above, and, where required, ask for consent inside the App before anything changes.
